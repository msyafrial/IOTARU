# Smart Office Solution

Create a productive, efficient, and safe workplace with IoT-powered office management. Monitor occupancy, optimize energy usage, and ensure employee well-being — all from a centralized dashboard.

## What's Included

- **Occupancy Sensors** — Real-time desk and room utilization tracking
- **Climate Control** — Automated HVAC management based on occupancy and preferences
- **Smart Lighting** — Daylight-adaptive lighting with occupancy-based control
- **Air Quality Monitor** — CO2, PM2.5, VOC, and temperature monitoring
- **Access Control** — Badge-based entry with visitor management

## Key Benefits

| Benefit | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Cost Reduction** | Cut energy costs by 25-40% with smart climate and lighting |
| **Space Optimization** | Data-driven insights on room and desk utilization |
| **Employee Safety** | Real-time air quality alerts and emergency notifications |
| **Compliance** | Automated logging for health and safety regulations |
| **Analytics Dashboard** | Visual reports on space usage, energy, and environmental metrics |

## How It Works

1. **Assess** — We analyze your office layout and identify key monitoring points
2. **Deploy** — Wireless sensors are installed without disrupting operations
3. **Connect** — All devices link to a central gateway and cloud dashboard
4. **Optimize** — AI-driven automation adjusts environment based on real-time data

## Ideal For

- Corporate offices and co-working spaces
- Facilities managers looking to reduce operational costs
- Companies focused on employee wellness and sustainability
