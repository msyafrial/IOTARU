# Smart Home Solution

Transform your home into an intelligent, connected living space. Our Smart Home solution integrates security, comfort, and energy management into one seamless platform.

## What's Included

- **Central Hub** — The brain of your smart home, connects all devices via Zigbee, Z-Wave, BLE, and Wi-Fi
- **Smart Sensors** — Motion, door/window, temperature, humidity, and air quality sensors
- **Smart Locks** — Biometric and app-controlled door locks with access logs
- **Smart Lighting** — Automated lighting scenes with scheduling and ambient control
- **Energy Monitor** — Real-time energy consumption tracking with anomaly alerts

## How It Works

1. **Install** — Our certified technicians set up your hub and devices in one visit
2. **Connect** — Devices auto-pair with the hub via mesh networking
3. **Automate** — Set up routines and rules through the mobile app
4. **Monitor** — Track everything from your dashboard in real-time
