# IOTARU Smart Smoke Detector

Next-generation smoke detector that goes beyond traditional alarms with smart sensors, instant mobile notifications, and automatic self-testing to ensure your home is always protected.

## Features

- **Dual-Sensor Technology** - Photoelectric + ionization for fast and slow fires
- **Real-Time Mobile Alerts** - Instant notifications even when you're away
- **Self-Testing** - Automatically tests sensors weekly and reports status
- **10-Year Battery** - Sealed lithium battery lasts the full device lifetime
- **Voice Alerts** - Announces the type and location of danger
