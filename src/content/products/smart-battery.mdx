# IOTARU Battery Monitor System

Keep your home battery storage in peak condition. Monitor state-of-charge, health metrics, and predicted lifespan with intelligent automation for backup power management.

## Features

- **State of Health** - Real-time battery degradation tracking
- **Cycle Counter** - Track charge/discharge cycles accurately
- **Backup Management** - Auto-switch to battery during outages
- **Cost Calculator** - Track savings from stored energy usage
- **Push Alerts** - Notifications for low battery or health warnings

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Compatibility | LiFePO4, Li-ion, Lead-acid |
| Voltage Range | 12V - 48V DC |
| Current Sense | Up to 200A shunt |
| Accuracy | +/- 0.5% voltage, +/- 1% current |
| Connectivity | Bluetooth 5.0 / Wi-Fi |
