# IOTARU Smart Parking Sensor

Park perfectly every time. Ultrasonic sensors guide you into your garage with LED indicators and stop you at the optimal position, while the app tracks which spaces are occupied.

## Features

- **Ultrasonic Distance Sensing** - Precise distance measurement to 0.1cm
- **LED Guidance Bar** - Green-yellow-red traffic light indicator
- **Mobile App** - Shows space availability in real-time
- **Multi-Car Support** - Tracks up to 4 parking spaces
- **Anti-Collision Alert** - Buzzer alert when approaching obstacles

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Range | 0.2m - 5.0m |
| Accuracy | +/- 1cm |
| Sensors | 2-4 per vehicle |
| Connectivity | Zigbee 3.0 |
| Power | CR2450 Battery (2 year life) |
