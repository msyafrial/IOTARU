# IOTARU Smart EV Charger

Charge your electric vehicle intelligently. This smart charger automatically balances your home's electrical load, integrates with solar panels, and schedules charging during off-peak hours for maximum savings.

## Features

- **Load Balancing** - Automatically adjusts charging to prevent overload
- **Solar Integration** - Prioritizes solar energy for green charging
- **Scheduled Charging** - Set charging windows for off-peak rates
- **Energy Dashboard** - Track charging costs and energy usage
- **OCPP Compatible** - Works with all major EV brands
