# IOTARU Smart Security Display

The command center for your smart security system. View all camera feeds, manage alerts, and control connected devices from one elegant touchscreen display mounted on your wall.

## Features

- **10.1-inch Touchscreen** - Full HD IPS display with wide viewing angles
- **Live Camera Feeds** - View up to 4 camera streams simultaneously
- **Zone Management** - Arm, disarm, and customize security zones
- **Smart Home Hub** - Controls lights, locks, and sensors from one panel
- **Emergency Button** - One-touch emergency alert to authorities
