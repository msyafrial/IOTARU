# IOTARU Smart Camera

High-definition AI-powered security camera designed for comprehensive property monitoring. Features intelligent motion detection, night vision, and seamless integration with the IOTARU ecosystem.

## Features

- **AI Motion Detection** - Distinguishes between people, vehicles, and animals to reduce false alarms
- **4K Ultra HD Recording** - Crystal clear video quality with 4K resolution
- **Night Vision** - Advanced infrared night vision up to 30 meters
- **Two-Way Audio** - Built-in speaker and microphone for remote communication
- **Cloud & Local Storage** - Supports both cloud recording and local SD card storage
