# IOTARU Air Quality Monitor

Know exactly what you're breathing. This 6-in-1 sensor tracks all critical air quality metrics and provides a simple health index score so you can keep your indoor environment safe and comfortable.

## Features

- **6-in-1 Sensors** - PM2.5, CO2, TVOC, temperature, humidity, and formaldehyde
- **Health Index Score** - Simple 0-100 rating for quick assessment
- **Color LED Display** - Visual air quality indicator at a glance
- **Historical Data** - Track trends over hours, days, and weeks
- **Auto Ventilation** - Triggers fans or windows when air quality drops

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Sensors | PM2.5, CO2, TVOC, HCHO, Temp, Humidity |
| Display | 3.5" color IPS + LED ring |
| Accuracy | +/- 5% for PM2.5, +/- 50ppm for CO2 |
| Connectivity | Wi-Fi / Bluetooth 5.0 |
| Power | USB-C / Battery (8 hours) |
