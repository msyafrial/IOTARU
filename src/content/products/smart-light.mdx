# IOTARU Smart Light

Adaptive lighting system that adjusts color temperature and brightness based on time of day and occupancy. Save energy while maintaining optimal lighting conditions.

## Features

- **Circadian Rhythm** - Automatically adjusts color temperature throughout the day
- **Occupancy Detection** - Lights turn on/off based on presence
- **Scene Control** - Pre-configured lighting scenes for different activities
- **Energy Monitoring** - Track energy consumption per fixture
- **Group Control** - Manage multiple lights as zones
