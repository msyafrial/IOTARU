# IOTARU Smart Irrigation System

Intelligent irrigation that saves water while keeping your garden perfectly green. Combines weather data, soil moisture, and zone scheduling for optimal watering efficiency.

## Features

- **Weather Adaptive** - Adjusts watering based on forecast data
- **Soil Moisture Sensing** - Real-time soil conditions per zone
- **8-Zone Support** - Independent scheduling for up to 8 zones
- **Water Usage Reports** - Track consumption and savings
- **Rain Skip** - Automatically skips watering when rain is detected

| Feature | Detail |
