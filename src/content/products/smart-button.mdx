# IOTARU Smart Button Controller

A single button that controls your entire smart home. Create custom scenes for one click, double click, and long press to control lights, locks, music, and more.

## Features

- **Three Actions** - Single click, double click, and long press
- **Scene Activation** - Trigger complex multi-device scenes instantly
- **Wireless & Portable** - Stick it anywhere or carry with you
- **Battery Life** - Up to 3 years on a single coin battery
- **Compact Design** - Minimalist 45mm diameter design

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Actions | Single / Double / Long Press |
| Battery | CR2032 (3 year life) |
