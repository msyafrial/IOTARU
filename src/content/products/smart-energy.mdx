# IOTARU Smart Energy

Enables organizations to monitor, analyze, and optimize energy consumption with precision. Identify inefficiencies, reduce operational costs, and support sustainability initiatives.

## Features

- **Real-Time Energy Monitoring** - Track energy usage in real time by department, equipment, or facility through a centralized dashboard
- **Actionable Efficiency Insights** - Advanced analytics provide clear recommendations to reduce energy waste
- **Cost Reduction** - Identify and eliminate energy inefficiencies
- **Sustainability Support** - Support green initiatives with data-driven decisions
