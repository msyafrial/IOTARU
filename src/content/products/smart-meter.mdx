# IOTARU Smart Meter

Precision energy monitoring device designed for commercial buildings. Track energy consumption across multiple circuits with real-time analytics and automated reporting.

## Features

- **Multi-Circuit Monitoring** - Track up to 32 circuits simultaneously
- **Real-Time Analytics** - Live dashboard with consumption trends
- **Automated Reporting** - Daily, weekly, and monthly energy reports
- **Anomaly Detection** - AI-powered alerts for unusual consumption patterns
- **Integration** - Export data to BMS and energy management systems

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Circuits | Up to 32 channels |
| Accuracy | ±0.5% |
| Sampling Rate | 1kHz |
| Connectivity | Ethernet / Modbus TCP |
| Display | 7" touchscreen |
