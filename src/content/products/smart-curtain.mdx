# IOTARU Smart Curtain Motor

Effortlessly control your curtains with whisper-quiet motorization. Schedule opening and closing, respond to sunlight levels, or use voice commands for hands-free convenience.

## Features

- **Ultra-Quiet Motor** - Below 35dB operating noise
- **Sunlight Sensor** - Auto-adjusts based on natural light intensity
- **Position Memory** - Remembers your preferred curtain positions
- **Voice Compatible** - Works with Alexa, Google Home, and Siri
- **Manual Override** - Gentle pull to trigger automatic movement
