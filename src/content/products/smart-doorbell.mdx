# IOTARU Smart Video Doorbell

Advanced video doorbell powered by AI that identifies visitors, detects packages, and sends instant alerts to your phone. Never miss a visitor or delivery again.

## Features

- **AI Person Detection** - Distinguishes between people, packages, and animals
- **1080p HD Video** - Crystal clear video with HDR for challenging lighting
- **Two-Way Audio** - Talk to visitors remotely through built-in speaker
- **Night Vision** - Full-color night vision for 24/7 monitoring
- **Package Detection** - Alerts when packages are delivered or picked up
