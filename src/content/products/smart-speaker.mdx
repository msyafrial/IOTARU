# IOTARU Smart Speaker

Voice-controlled smart speaker with built-in hub capabilities. Manage your entire IoT ecosystem with simple voice commands while enjoying premium audio quality.

## Features

- **Voice Control** - Hands-free management of all connected devices
- **Multi-Room Audio** - Synchronize music across multiple speakers
- **Built-in Hub** - Direct control of Zigbee and Z-Wave devices
- **Noise Cancellation** - Advanced far-field voice recognition
- **Privacy Mode** - Physical microphone disconnect for privacy

## Specifications

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Speakers | 2x 10W woofers + 2x tweeters |
| Microphones | 7-mic array |
| Connectivity | Wi-Fi 6 / Bluetooth 5.2 |
| Hub Protocols | Zigbee 3.0 / Thread |
| Power | 12V DC adapter |
