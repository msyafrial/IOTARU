# IOTARU Smart Weather Station

Get professional-grade weather data from your own backyard. Track wind speed, rainfall, UV index, barometric pressure, and more with this all-in-one outdoor weather station.

## Features

- **All-in-One Sensors** - Wind, rain, UV, temperature, humidity, pressure
- **Hyperlocal Data** - Weather conditions specific to your exact location
- **24-Hour Forecast** - AI-powered predictions based on local trends
- **Historical Analysis** - Compare current and past weather patterns
- **Solar Powered** - Self-sustaining with solar panel and battery backup
