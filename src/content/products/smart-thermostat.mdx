# IOTARU Smart Thermostat

Intelligent climate control system that learns your preferences and optimizes energy usage. Reduce energy costs while maintaining perfect comfort in any environment.

## Features

- **Learning Algorithm** - Adapts to your schedule and temperature preferences automatically
- **Remote Control** - Manage temperature from anywhere via mobile app
- **Energy Reports** - Detailed analytics on energy usage and savings
- **Geofencing** - Automatically adjusts when you leave or arrive home
- **Multi-Zone Support** - Control different areas independently
