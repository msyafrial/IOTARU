# IOTARU Solar Energy Monitor

Maximize your solar investment with real-time monitoring and intelligent analytics. Track energy production, consumption, and grid export to optimize your return on investment.

## Features

- **Real-Time Production** - Live solar generation output monitoring
- **Grid Export Tracking** - See exactly how much energy you sell back
- **ROI Dashboard** - Track savings, payback period, and carbon offset
- **Panel-Level Monitoring** - Identify underperforming individual panels
- **Alert System** - Notifications for production drops or system faults
